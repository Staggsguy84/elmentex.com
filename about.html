About ElmenTex

I’m Andy, based in the San Antonio / Elmendorf, Texas area. ElmenTex Creations is my small shop focused on turning ideas into physical pieces using 3D printing, laser engraving/cutting, and CNC routing.

Whether you have a finished file, a photo, a sketch, or just a description of what you want, I can help bring it to life — custom one-offs, gifts, functional parts, signs, and more.

The work is done locally with attention to detail and a preference for wood, acrylic, and durable materials.
